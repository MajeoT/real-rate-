# Real Rate

Log hours worked, hours paid and your hourly rate each day. Real Rate shows what you actually earn per hour worked, by day, week, month, year or any custom period.

Data is saved in your browser only. Use "Backup and restore" on the page to keep a copy.

## Install on your phone

- **Android (Chrome):** open the site and tap **Install app** at the top, or use the menu (⋮) and choose **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the site, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

It opens full screen like an app and works without signal. Your data stays in that phone's browser, so use Backup and restore to move it.

## Building

`index.html` is built from the Claude artifact page: `python3 build.py path/to/real-rate.html`
